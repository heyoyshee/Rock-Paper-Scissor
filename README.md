# ✊ ✋ ✌️ Rock Paper Scissors

A simple Rock Paper Scissors game where you play against the computer. Pick your move, the computer picks randomly, and the winner is decided by the classic rules.

## Rules

- **Rock** beats **Scissors**
- **Scissors** beats **Paper**
- **Paper** beats **Rock**
- Same choice from both sides is a **tie**

## Features

- Play against the computer, which chooses its move at random
- Instant result for every round (win, lose, or tie)
- Score tracking across rounds
- Option to play again without restarting

## How to Play

1. Start the game.
2. Choose **Rock**, **Paper**, or **Scissors**.
3. The computer makes its choice.
4. The winner of the round is shown.
5. Keep playing or quit whenever you like.
